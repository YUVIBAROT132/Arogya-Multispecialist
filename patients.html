Bring with you

  • Photo ID
  • Old reports and prescriptions
  • Insurance card, if you have one
  • List of current medicines

Visitor rules

Two visitors per patient in wards, one in ICU. Children under 12 are not allowed in wards. Please wash your hands and keep phones silent.

Your rights and responsibilities

You have the right to

  • Respectful, non-discriminatory care
  • Clear information about your condition and options
  • Privacy and confidentiality of your records
  • A second opinion and copies of your records
  • Raise a concern and get a response

We ask you to

  • Share complete and accurate health information
  • Follow the treatment plan, or tell us if you cannot
  • Respect other patients, staff and hospital property
  • Settle bills as agreed in your estimate
