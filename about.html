About Arogya

A neighbourhood hospital built on three promises: explain clearly, treat safely, charge fairly.

One roof for the whole family

Arogya began as a 30-bed clinic with a simple idea: patients should not have to travel between buildings to get answers. Today it is a 150-bed multispecialty hospital with eight departments, a 24-hour emergency room, diagnostics and a pharmacy in the same campus.

Every patient has a named doctor, a written plan and a clear estimate of cost before any planned admission.

What guides our work

Explain clearly

Plain words, written plans and time for questions.

Treat safely

Checklists, hand hygiene and double-checks on every medicine.

Charge fairly

Written estimates and itemised bills with no surprises.

Treat with dignity

Privacy, respect and family involvement in every decision.

Our journey

  3. 2016
    New block with ICU, NICU and three operation theatres.
  4. 2019
    Imaging centre adds CT and MRI.

    Online appointments and digital reports begin.
  6. Today
    150 beds and eight departments.
